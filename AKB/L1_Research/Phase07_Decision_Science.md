## Scope notes

- Select patterns by organizational intent, constraints, evidence quality, economics, and risk tolerance.
- No universally best topology / framework.
- Promote to L2 only with evidence, applicability, counterexamples, confidence, version, owner, review date.

## Research prompts

1. What recurring organizational problems appear in this domain?
2. What failure modes and blast radii are documented?
3. What evaluation criteria distinguish good from fragile designs?
4. What tradeoffs are irreducible (cost/quality/latency/safety)?
5. What evidence would falsify a popular pattern here?
